Endless Walk

A first-person walk that never ends. The route ahead is generated as you move, and every walk is unique to its seed. Keep an eye out for glowing landmarks: those are photoreal 3D captures placed along the trail.

Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to walk, mouse to look (click to capture the cursor), T toggles auto-walk, P pauses.
Mobile: left stick to walk, drag right side to look.
